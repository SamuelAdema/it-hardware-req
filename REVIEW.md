Strengths

1. Clear purpose and user flow: the home page, searchable hardware catalog, and requisition dashboard make it easy to understand how users request equipment.
2. Useful catalog details: products display categories, availability, images, and prices, helping users compare hardware before submitting a request.
3. Good user experience: the interface separates browsing from authenticated requisition management, with a dedicated sign-in flow for protected requests.

Weaknesses

1. Limited visible workflow: the public pages do not fully demonstrate the complete approval, fulfillment, and tracking process without signing in.
2. Limited product information: the catalog could provide more detailed specifications, compatibility information, and clearer product comparisons.
3. Authentication and authorization need deeper verification: protected pages and server-side access controls should be tested to ensure users can access only their own requisitions and authorized actions.
